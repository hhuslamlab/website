abstract: 'Machine learning (ML) and artificial intelligence applications in speech analysis have advanced significantly in recent years, warranting dedicated examination in the context of stuttering research. This chapter explores the emerging field of ML applications to stuttered speech, addressing its growing importance within stuttering studies and its potential to enhance both research methodologies and clinical interventions.

Previously, stuttering research has primarily relied on human expertise for the identification and classification of disfluencies. The integration of ML techniques offers the potential for more objective, consistent, and scalable methods of analysis. While existing literature has extensively covered the identification and sub-classification of stutters from audio signals using ML this chapter aims to elucidate the current challenges and potential solutions within this interdisciplinary domain of ML and stuttering research.

The application of ML to stuttered speech presents unique difficulties due to the complex nature of the disorder and the variability in its manifestation across individuals. These challenges include, but are not limited to, the acquisition of sufficiently large and accent and fluency diverse datasets, the development of robust algorithms capable of distinguishing subtle speech characteristics, and the integration of contextual information in analysis. These and other roadblocks have led to a discordance between the field of research and the field of practice.

This chapter will examine these roadblocks, proposing potential solutions and discussing their implications for advancing stuttering research and treatment. Furthermore, it will explore how overcoming these challenges could lead to significant improvements for people who stutter and speech-language therapists. The integration of ML techniques with traditional speech-language pathology approaches has the potential to uncover novel patterns and relationships within stuttered speech, potentially leading to more effective interventions and a deeper understanding of the underlying mechanisms of stuttering. Potential advancements include more precise diagnostic tools, personalised therapeutic approaches, and real-time fluency aids.'
